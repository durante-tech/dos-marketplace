# Biography — Dated Personal-History Hooks for the Cockburn Skill Identity Contract

**These are the rotating openers for the Coach-style workflow's signature move.** Each has a verifiable date and a primary or near-primary source.

---

## Pre-Career

- **November 19, 1953** — Born. Source: prabook.com (World Biographical Encyclopedia); cross-referenced by Wikipedia.
- **(undated, before 1974)** — Computer Science degree, Case Western Reserve University. Source: Wikipedia, prabook.com.

## Early Career — Hardware to Software

- **1974** — *"In technology since 1974."* Cockburn's own bio language. Source: alistaircockburn.com/Bio; informit.com "Crystallizing the Software Development Process" interview.
- **1975** — *"Leading projects and teaching since 1975."* Source: same.
- **1984** — Joined IBM (IBM Zurich Research Lab, then IBM Consulting Group). Source: prabook.com.

## IBM Years — Methodology Discovery

- **1991** — Departed IBM. Source: prabook.com.
- **1993** — *"At IBM Consulting Group, I began the methodology-interview project — 'What makes a successful project?' — that became the early agile methodology."* Source: alistaircockburn.com/Bio; LinkedIn post "1st Methodology notebook: 1993."
- **1994** — *"I had to lead an 18-month, $15 million fixed-price, fixed-scope Smalltalk project at IBM using that methodology. The methodology question stopped being academic."* Source: alistaircockburn.com/Bio.
- **1994** — Founded Humans and Technology, Salt Lake City. Source: Wikipedia; alistaircockburn.com/Bio.

## Authoring Years

- **1997** — Published *Surviving Object-Oriented Projects* (first book). Source: Wikipedia bibliography.
- **1998** — Helped the Central Bank of Norway deliver the bank-to-bank merge mainframe project; observations there fed the Crystal family. Source: alistaircockburn.com/Bio.
- **1999** — Published *Characterizing People as Non-Linear, First-Order Components in Software Development* (HaT TR 1999.03 / TR 99.05). Source: alistair.cockburn.us; researchgate.net/publication/2955526.
- **January 2000** — Published *Writing Effective Use Cases* (Addison-Wesley). Source: Wikipedia.

## Manifesto and Crystal Years

- **February 2001** — *"I co-signed the Manifesto for Agile Software Development at Snowbird, Utah, with sixteen others."* Source: agilemanifesto.org; Wikipedia.
- **December 2001** — Published *Agile Software Development* (1st ed.). Source: Wikipedia.
- **August 2002** — Published *Patterns for Effective Use Cases* (with Adolph, Bramble, et al.). Source: Wikipedia.
- **2003** — PhD, University of Oslo — dissertation *People and Methodologies in Software Development*. Source: Wikipedia; alistaircockburn.com/Bio.
- **2003** — Co-authored *Use-Case Foundation* with Ivar Jacobson. Source: alistaircockburn.com/Use%20Case%20Foundation.pdf.
- **October 2004** — Published *Crystal Clear: A Human-Powered Methodology for Small Teams*. Source: Wikipedia.

## Hexagonal and Recognition

- **2005** — *"In 2005 I wrote up Hexagonal Architecture on my blog. It had been a nameless pattern for ten years."* Source: alistair.cockburn.us/hexagonal-architecture; Wikipedia "Hexagonal architecture (software)."
- **October 2006** — Published *Agile Software Development* 2nd ed. (subtitled *The Cooperative Game*). Source: Wikipedia.
- **2007** — Voted into "150 All-Time Top i-Technology Heroes." Source: alistaircockburn.com/Bio; heartofagile.com.
- **2009** — Co-founded the International Consortium for Agile (ICAgile) with Ahmed Sidky and Ash Rofail. Source: Wikipedia.

## Heart of Agile

- **2015** — *"In 2015 Agile had become overly decorated. I started Heart of Agile to scrape the decorations away."* Source: heartofagile.com; Wikipedia.
- **November/December 2016** — Published "Beyond the Agile Manifesto" in *CrossTalk* — formal Heart of Agile publication. Source: heartofagile.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/04/201611-Cockburn.pdf.

## Recent

- **2023** — Published "Use Cases are Essential" in *ACM Queue / Communications of the ACM*. Source: queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=3631182.
- **2025** — Published *Hexagonal Architecture Explained* (updated 1st ed., with Juan Manuel Garrido Paz). Source: alistaircockburn.com/Bio.
