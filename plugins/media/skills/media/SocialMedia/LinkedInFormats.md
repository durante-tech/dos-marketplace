# LinkedIn Image Formats (2025-2026)

Complete reference for all LinkedIn image types, dimensions, and specifications.

## Personal Profile

| Format | Recommended px | Min px | Aspect Ratio | Max Size | Notes |
|--------|---------------|--------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Profile Photo | 400x400 | 300x300 | 1:1 (circular crop) | 8MB | Center face with ~60% facial coverage |
| Profile Banner | 1584x396 | 1128x191 | 4:1 | 8MB | Bottom-left 568x264 obscured by profile photo on desktop. Safe: 1200x300 centered. |

## Feed Posts

| Format | Recommended px | Min px | Aspect Ratio | Max Size | Notes |
|--------|---------------|--------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Landscape | 1200x627 | 640x360 | 1.91:1 | 10MB | Best for link shares |

| Portrait | 1080x1350 | 360x640 | 4:5 | 10MB | Max feed real estate on mobile |

## Document Carousel (PDF/PPTX)

| Format | Recommended px | Aspect Ratio | Max Size | Notes |
|--------|---------------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Square | 1080x1080 | 1:1 | 100MB | Safe default for all devices |
| Portrait | 1080x1350 | 4:5 | 100MB | BEST for mobile. 8-12 slides sweet spot. |
| Landscape | 1280x720 | 16:9 | 100MB | Presentation-style content |

Max 300 pages. Formats: PDF, PPTX, DOCX. Keep under 10MB for mobile loading.

## Strategic Notes

- **1200x627 (1.91:1)** is LinkedIn's universal workhorse — covers feed, links, articles, ads, jobs
- **4:5 (1080x1350)** portrait is the mobile engagement winner — more screen real estate
- **Document carousels** (PDF at 1080x1350) are the highest-engagement organic format right now
- Logos should work on both light and dark backgrounds (dark mode)
