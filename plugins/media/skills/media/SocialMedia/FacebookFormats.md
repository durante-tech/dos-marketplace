# Facebook Image Formats (2025-2026)

Complete reference for all Facebook image types, dimensions, and specifications.

## Profile & Cover Images

| Format | Recommended px | Min px | Aspect Ratio | Max Size | Notes |
|--------|---------------|--------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Profile Picture | 400x400 | 180x180 | 1:1 (circular crop) | 8MB | Desktop: 170x170, Mobile: 128x128 |
| Personal Cover | 851x315 | 400x150 | 2.7:1 | 8MB | Safe zone: 640x312 centered. Mobile crops sides. |
| Page Cover | 851x315 | 400x150 | 2.7:1 | 8MB | Same as personal. CTA button overlays bottom. |
| Group Cover | 1640x856 | 1024x512 | 1.91:1 | 8MB | Desktop crops bottom ~194px. Design in top 662px. |
| Event Cover | 1920x1005 | 1200x628 | 1.91:1 | 8MB | No text in bottom 15% (event details overlay). |

## Feed Posts (Organic)

| Format | Recommended px | Min px | Aspect Ratio | Max Size | Notes |
|--------|---------------|--------|--------------|----------|-------|
| Landscape | 1200x628 | 600x315 | 1.91:1 | 8MB | Good for link-style posts |
| Square | 1080x1080 | 600x600 | 1:1 | 8MB | Versatile, works across placements |
| Portrait | 1080x1350 | 600x750 | 4:5 | 8MB | BEST engagement. Max vertical ratio in feed. |
| Carousel (per card) | 1080x1080 | 600x600 | 1:1 | 8MB/card | Up to 10 cards. All same ratio. |
| Shared Link Preview | 1200x630 | 600x315 | 1.91:1 | 8MB | From og:image. Below 600x315 = small thumbnail. |

## Stories & Reels

| Format | Recommended px | Min px | Aspect Ratio | Max Size | Safe Zone |
|--------|---------------|--------|--------------|----------|-----------|
| Story | 1080x1920 | 600x1067 | 9:16 | 8MB | Top 250px + bottom 340px = UI overlays |
| Reel | 1080x1920 | 600x1067 | 9:16 | 4GB | All video is now Reels (June 2025+) |
| Reel Cover/Thumbnail | 1080x1920 | - | 9:16 (crops to 4:5 in grid) | - | Center key content vertically |

|--------|---------------|--------------|-------|
| OG Image | 1200x630 | 1.91:1 | Universal for link previews across platforms |
| Video Thumbnail (landscape) | 1200x675 | 16:9 | Match video aspect ratio |

## Strategic Notes

- **4:5 portrait (1080x1350)** consistently outperforms other ratios for feed engagement
- Since June 2025, **all video is published as Reels** — no separate video post format
- Right column ads dropped 1.91:1 support — **1:1 only** now
- The 20% text rule is no longer enforced as a hard block, but less text = better distribution
