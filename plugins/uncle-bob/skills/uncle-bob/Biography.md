## Object Mentor and the Consulting Years

- **(c. 1990s)** — Co-founded and led **Object Mentor, Inc.** (with his son Micah Martin and others) — a training and
  consulting firm teaching object-oriented design, patterns, and XP/TDD. Source: Object Mentor history; Wikipedia.
- **(late 1990s–2000s)** — Articulated and popularized the **SOLID** principles (Single Responsibility, Open/Closed,
  Liskov Substitution, Interface Segregation, Dependency Inversion) — the acronym arrangement is his. Source: his
  Object Mentor articles; *Agile Software Development: Principles, Patterns, and Practices*.

## The Books

- **1995** — Published *Designing Object-Oriented C++ Applications Using the Booch Method*. Source: Wikipedia
  bibliography. `[unverified — year]`
- **2002** — Published *Agile Software Development: Principles, Patterns, and Practices* (the "PPP" book — the SOLID
  principles in book form). Source: Wikipedia bibliography.
- **August 2008** — Published *Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship* (Prentice Hall) — his most
  widely-read book. Source: publisher record; Wikipedia.
- **2011** — Published *The Clean Coder: A Code of Conduct for Professional Programmers*. Source: Wikipedia.
- **2017** — Published *Clean Architecture: A Craftsman's Guide to Software Structure and Design* (the Dependency
  Rule, screaming architecture). Source: Wikipedia.
- **2019** — Published *Clean Agile: Back to Basics* — a return to the Manifesto's original intent. Source: Wikipedia.
- **2021** — Published *Clean Craftsmanship: Disciplines, Standards, and Ethics*. Source: Wikipedia.
- **2023** — Published *Functional Design: Principles, Patterns, and Practices*. Source: publisher record.
