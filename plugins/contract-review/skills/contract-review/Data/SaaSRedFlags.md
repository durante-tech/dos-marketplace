# SaaS Contract Red Flags Reference

Quick reference for the agent team. These are the most common red flags in SaaS/service agreements from the client perspective.

## Severity Rubric (Shared Across All Agents)

| Rating | Meaning | Action Required |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| RED | Clause creates material risk, unfair asymmetry, or regulatory exposure | Must negotiate before signing |
| YELLOW | Clause is suboptimal but not dangerous; could become an issue | Flag for negotiation, acceptable if vendor won't budge |
| GREEN | Clause is standard market terms or client-favorable | No action needed |

## Common Red Flags by Category

### Risk
- Unlimited client liability, capped vendor liability
- One-way indemnification (client only)
- No termination for convenience
- Full remaining term payable on early termination
- Broad force majeure excusing SLAs
- Complete "AS IS" warranty disclaimer
- Foreign exclusive jurisdiction

### Commercial
- Hidden usage-based pricing
- Net 15 or prepay requirements
- Auto-renewal with uncapped price increases
- No uptime SLA or vague "best efforts"
- No SLA credits or remedies
- Scope defined by reference to "current website"
- Support costs extra with no response SLA

### Compliance
- No Data Processing Agreement
- Vendor claims ownership of client data
- Unlimited subprocessor discretion
- No audit rights
- Vendor owns all customizations/work product
- One-way confidentiality
- Non-compete restricting competitor use
- No data export/portability provision
